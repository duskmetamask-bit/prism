## WHAT THESE ACCOUNTS HAVE IN COMMON

### Hook Patterns (All Use at Least 3 of These)

| Pattern | Used By | Why It Works |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Specific numbers (not round) | All of them | "$127,493" beats "$100K" every time |
| Outcome first | levelsio, sanjaypworld, matt_gray | Hook states result before method |
| "I did X and got Y" | sanjaypworld, Dannypostmaa | Proof + curiosity gap |
| Bold contrarian claim | levelsio, matt_gray, Dannypostmaa | Challenges reader's beliefs |
| Specific timeframe | All of them | "48 hours" beats "a while" |
| "Most people don't know" | sanjaypworld | Authority + exclusivity |
| Diary/sequence format | indipage_ai | Narrative + consistency |
| "Here's the truth about X" | sanjaypworld, swyx | Authority + counter-narrative |

### What They DON'T Do

- ❌ Engagement bait ("tag someone who...")
- ❌ Generic motivational quotes
- ❌ Posting links in main tweet body
- ❌ Posting without a clear hook
- ❌ Using more than 2-3 hashtags
- ❌ Posting the same format every time (variety matters)
- ❌ Being vague ("great progress", "amazing results")

### Growth Levers (Ranked by Impact)

1. **Consistency** — Post every day or near-daily
2. **Specificity** — Real numbers, real names, real dates
3. **Controversy** — Have a point of view, even if half disagree
4. **Proof** — Show the work, show the results
5. **Format variety** — Mix threads, singles, photos, polls
6. **Engagement** — Reply to others, build community
7. **Visual** — Thumbnails, quote cards, photos

## SPECIFIC POST BREAKDOWNS

### Post Type 1: The Revenue Update (levelsio style)
**Format:** "Month N of [product]: $[specific] MRR — [1-sentence insight]"
**Why it works:** Proof + specificity + aspirational + consistent pattern
**Typical engagement:** 500-2000 likes
**Best for:** Indie hackers, SaaS founders, anyone with numbers to share

### Post Type 2: The Hot Take (levelsio/matt_gray style)
**Format:** "[Controversial claim about industry/tool/method]"
**Why it works:** Challenges beliefs, gets replies, shares from disagreement
**Typical engagement:** 200-1000 likes + high reply volume
**Best for:** Anyone with a contrarian view on their space

### Post Type 3: The Experiment (sanjaypworld style)
**Format:** "I tested [X] for [timeframe]. [Surprising outcome]."
**Why it works:** Curiosity gap + specific + real data
**Typical engagement:** 300-1500 likes
**Best for:** People who test things and have results to share

### Post Type 4: The Numbered Lesson (swyx style)
**Format:** "The [N] things I wish I knew before [significant event]"
**Why it works:** Specific + actionable + saves-friendly
**Typical engagement:** 200-800 likes + high saves
**Best for:** Career, business, technical content

### Post Type 5: The Ship Story (Dannypostmaa style)
**Format:** "Built [X] in [time]. [Outcome]."
**Why it works:** Speed + outcome + proof
**Typical engagement:** 150-600 likes
**Best for:** Builders, developers, makers

### Post Type 6: The Failure Confession (indipage_ai/matt_gray style)
**Format:** "I almost gave up at [specific moment]. [What changed]."
**Why it works:** Vulnerability + relatability + narrative arc
**Typical engagement:** 200-1000 likes
**Best for:** Anyone building something, founders, personal brand

## WHAT PRISM SHOULD LEARN FROM THIS

### The 7 Hooks PRISM Should Default To:

1. **Specific Revenue/Hit:** "[Product] hit $[specific number] MRR — [1 lesson]"
2. **Contrarian Claim:** "[Unpopular opinion about your space]"
3. **I Tested It:** "I spent [time] testing [thing]. [Surprising result]."
4. **Specific Lesson:** "The [N] things I learned [doing X]"
5. **I Almost Quit:** "[Moment I almost quit]. [Why I didn't]."
6. **Most People Don't Know:** "Most people don't know [specific insight]. [Here it is]."
7. **Bold Prediction:** "[Specific claim about future of your space]"

- 30% Personal story / building in public
- 25% Specific tips / how-to / lessons

- 10% Engagement / question / poll
