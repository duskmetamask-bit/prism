# CONTENT PRINCIPLES MASTER SYSTEM
**For:** prism content agent
**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL — Universal content laws

---

## THE CORE PREMISE

Every piece of content that performs well shares one thing:
**It makes the reader feel something OR learn something — ideally both.**

Content that neither teaches nor moves emotion dies. Algorithm or no algorithm.

---

## HOOK MECHANICS

### What a Hook Must Do (Pick One)
1. **Create curiosity** — they need to know more
2. **Trigger recognition** — "that's so true" or "that's me"
3. **Challenge a belief** — they've been doing it wrong
4. **Promise a specific outcome** — they'll be smarter/better/faster

### Hook Categories That Never Die

**Counterintuitive Hooks**
> "Stop chasing productivity. Here's what actually works."
> "Most of what you know about [topic] is backwards."
> "X is not the goal. Y is."

**Specificity Hooks**
> "I spent $47k testing ads. Here's what 90% wasted."
> "3am. 4 kids. This is how I built a 6-figure business."
> "The 11-word email that got a 67% reply rate."

**Recognition Hooks (Second-Person)**
> "You know that moment when [painful truth]?"
> "If you've ever [frustrating situation], this is for you."
> "You're not [wrong belief]. You're [right framing]."

**Authority Hooks**
> "After auditing 200+ businesses, here's what I found."
> "The research is clear on [common misconception]."
> "Top performers don't [common habit]. They [what they do instead]."

**Story Hooks**
> "Last Tuesday I almost [pivotal moment]. Then [discovery]."
> "I didn't believe [claim] until I [proof]."
> "The worst day of my career taught me [lesson]."

**Pattern Interrupt Hooks**
> "[Common assumption] is wrong. Here's why."
> "Everything you know about [topic] is a lie."
> "I'm going to say something controversial..."

### First Line Mechanics
- First line must STAND ALONE as a complete thought
- Never start with "In this video/post/today we'll..."
- Never open with a question you're not going to answer immediately
- 1-3 sentences max for opening

## CONTENT STRUCTURE FRAMEWORKS

### AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

**Attention:** Hook that stops the scroll
**Interest:** Build the problem or promise
**Desire:** Show proof, data, or emotional resonance
**Action:** Clear next step

### PAS (Problem → Agitation → Solution)

1. **Problem:** Name the pain specifically
2. **Agitation:** Make them feel the consequences of NOT solving it
3. **Solution:** Your content delivers this

### The Story Arc

```
[Setup]    → Who, what, context (3-5 sentences)
[Conflict] → What went wrong / what was wrong with the old way
[Turn]     → What changed / what you discovered
[Result]   → The outcome / the lesson / the win
[Apply]    → How they can apply the same principle
```

### Framework Posts (Listicles Done Right)

Generic lists fail. Lists with insight succeed.

**Formula:** [Number] + [Specific Outcome] + [Time/Effort Marker if applicable]

Bad: "5 Tips for Better Productivity"
Good: "5 Decisions That Cost Me 3 Years of Business Growth"

Each point must:
- Have a headline that promises value
- Have 2-4 sentences of substance
- Never be self-evident or generic advice

### Comparison Posts

**Structure:**
1. "Everyone says X is better. They're wrong (for this reason)."
2. Show specific dimensions of comparison
3. State when X wins, when Y wins
4. Give the decision framework

## MOMENTUM MECHANICS

### What Keeps People Reading/Watching

1. **Friction removal** — short sentences, simple words, active voice
2. **Specificity over vagueness** — real numbers, real names, real dates
3. **Pacing variation** — short punchy sentences followed by longer context
4. **Cliffhangers** — "...and that's when everything changed" (use sparingly)
5. **Pattern interrupts** — unexpected stat, contrarian claim mid-post

### Cliffhanger Mechanics (Threads)

Every tweet in a thread needs to answer "so what?" before moving on.

Don't:
> "Here's why most businesses fail at marketing. [2 hours later...]"

Do:
> "Here's why most businesses fail at marketing. The number one reason: they confuse activity with progress. [scroll]"

### Pacing by Format Length

| Format | Sentence Length | Paragraph Length | Action Per Block |
|--------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| X post | 1-2 sentences | N/A | One thought |
| Thread tweet | 2-4 sentences | 1 paragraph | One insight |
| LinkedIn post | 1-2 sentences | 2-3 lines | One sub-point |
| Newsletter | Mixed | 2-4 sentences | One paragraph |
| YouTube script | Mixed | 3-5 sentences | One beat |

## EMOTIONAL TRIGGER LIBRARY

### Emotions That Drive Sharing

**Anger (careful use)**
> "This advice is destroying how you [outcome]. Stop."
Use when: anger is justified, targets a shared frustration

**Aspiration**
> "People who [behavior] always [outcome]."
Use when: your audience wants to be in a certain category

**Recognition ("That's me")**
> "If you [behavior], you're probably [limiting belief]."
Use when: surfacing hidden patterns your audience lives

**Curiosity**
> "The [profession] I never wanted to become."
Use when: setup creates genuine mystery

**Exclusivity / Insider**
> "Here's what nobody talks about [topic]."
Use when: you have genuinely insider knowledge

### Emotions That Kill Sharing

- Pure fear (not actionable)
- Victim mentality
- Nihilism ("nothing matters anyway")
- Advice that doesn't apply to anyone real

## CALL TO ACTION MECHANICS

### When to Place CTA

| Format | CTA Placement |
|--------|--------------|
| X single post | End (1 CTA max) |
| X thread | End of thread + optional in-thread ("save this") |
| LinkedIn post | End + first comment (1 day later) |
| Newsletter | End (1 primary, 1 secondary max) |
| YouTube | End + pinned comment + description |

### CTA Formulas That Work

**For Engagement:**
- "Quote retweet the part that hit you." (X)
- "Drop your [topic] struggle in the comments." (YouTube/LinkedIn)
- "Save this for when you need it." (all platforms)

**For Action:**
- "Link in comments to [relevant resource]."
- "DM me 'audit' and I'll [value exchange]."
- "Share this with someone who needs to hear it."

**For Follow:**
- "Follow for more [topic content]."
- "Next week I'll cover [next topic] — stay tuned."

### CTA Mistakes That Kill

- CTA that asks for too much (don't ask for a share AND comment AND follow)
- CTA placed before content delivers value
- CTA that feels transactional, not genuine
- CTA that is vague ("let me know your thoughts")

## CONTENT QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before publishing any piece, verify:

**Hook:**
- [ ] First line stops the scroll (not just describes the content)
- [ ] The promise is specific, not generic

**Substance:**
- [ ] At least one insight, data point, or perspective the audience can't get elsewhere
- [ ] Not just regurgitating common knowledge
- [ ] Specific examples over abstract principles

**Structure:**
- [ ] One idea per section / tweet / slide
- [ ] Flows logically — each piece leads to the next
- [ ] Ending doesn't peter out — strong closer

**Format:**
- [ ] Appropriate length for platform
- [ ] Scannable (paragraphs, bullets, numbered lists)
- [ ] Key terms bolded where appropriate

**CTA:**
- [ ] Clear, single action
- [ ] Placed after value is delivered

## CONTENT REPURPOSING HIERARCHY

One piece of pillar content can become:

```
[LONG-FORM YOUTUBE / NEWSLETTER]
    ↓
[LinkedIn post] — key insight + personal angle
[LinkedIn carousel] — step-by-step breakdown
[X thread] — 5-7 tweet breakdown of key points
[X single post] — one punchy insight
[Quote card] — most quotable line
[YouTube Short] — hook + one insight from video
```

Rules:
- Adapt for platform (never cross-post identical content)
- Each version must feel native to that platform
- Lead with the strongest platform-native format

## WHAT FAILS (ANTI-PATTERNS)

1. **Vague hooks** — "Some thoughts on..." instead of the actual thought
2. **No specificity** — "You should be more productive" vs "Do these 3 things before 9am"
3. **Broadcast mentality** — talking AT audience vs WITH them
4. **Generic advice** — what anyone could Google in 30 seconds
5. **No personality** — sounds like a corporate press release
6. **Firesale CTA** — every post is a pitch
7. **No proof** — claims without evidence or example
8. **Clutter** — too many hashtags, tags, emojis, links in one post
9. **Setup without payoff** — story that doesn't deliver the lesson
10. **Chasing trends** — posting about everything viral instead of your authority area
